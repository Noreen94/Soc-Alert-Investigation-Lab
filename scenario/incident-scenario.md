# SOC Investigation Scenario: Suspicious Login Activity

## 1. Organisation Background

This investigation involves a fictional financial services company called FinTrust Solutions.

The organisation has 150 employees who use Microsoft 365 to access company email and business applications.

## 2. Security Alert

The IT security team receives an alert indicating multiple failed login attempts against an employee account, followed by a successful login.

## 3. Reported Events

The following activity has been identified:

- Multiple unsuccessful authentication attempts.
- A successful login after repeated failures.
- Login activity originating from an unfamiliar IP address.
- The account owner does not recognise the activity.

## 4. Investigation Objectives

As the SOC Analyst, your responsibilities are to:

1. Review the authentication logs.
2. Identify suspicious login patterns.
3. Establish whether the activity indicates a potential brute-force or password-spraying attack.
4. Determine whether the account may have been compromised.
5. Recommend appropriate containment and remediation actions.

## 5. Scope

This is a simulated investigation using fictional data. No real employee accounts or company systems are involved.

## 6. Expected Deliverables

- Authentication log analysis.
- Investigation notes.
- Evidence-based findings.
- Incident severity assessment.
- Incident response report.
